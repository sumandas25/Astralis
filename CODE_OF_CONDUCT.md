# Code of Conduct

Astralis is an open-source learning project. Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews is expected to help keep the space respectful and useful.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and constructive.
- Assume good intent, especially with new contributors.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and explain decisions when you can.
- Respect different skill levels, backgrounds, and perspectives.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insults, threats, or deliberately hostile comments.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeatedly derailing project discussions.
- Spam or low-effort self-promotion.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this code. Repeated or severe behavior may result in being blocked from participating.

If you need to report a concern, contact the maintainers privately when possible.
